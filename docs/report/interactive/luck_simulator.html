How luck fakes skill

Two small models behind Why beating the market is so hard, from the Atlas trading research project. Nothing here uses real prices: every bot below is pure chance, which is the point.

1. Test enough random bots and one looks like a genius

Each bot's daily return is the market's plus random noise: none has any real skill. Pick the one with the best past record, then watch its next year.

2. How many years it takes to tell a real edge from luck

A strategy that beats the market by a little, while wandering away from it by a lot, needs a very long record before its edge is two standard errors from zero: years = (2 × tracking error / edge)².
